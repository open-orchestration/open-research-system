This finding grounds the three canonical multi-hop question-answering benchmarks — HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue — on their primary papers, covering how each dataset is constructed and how each is scored. It opens a new corner of domain 16 (evaluation benchmarks): where the eval-regime, RAGAS, and GAIA findings cover RAG-metric formulas and agentic task suites, this one covers the multi-hop QA family specifically — datasets whose defining property is that answering requires chaining evidence across multiple documents, with per-sentence or per-triple supporting-evidence supervision attached to every example.

## HotpotQA

HotpotQA is a dataset of 113k Wikipedia-based question–answer pairs, with 112,779 valid examples collected in total [ce34e3bd9]. It contains two reasoning types: bridge questions, which require inferring a bridge entity to connect two paragraphs and complete a second-hop question (42% of the analyzed set), and comparison questions, which compare two entities (27%) [ce34e3bd9]. Questions are crowdsourced on Amazon Mechanical Turk: candidate paragraph pairs are generated by extracting hyperlinks from the first paragraphs of Wikipedia articles and sampling connected paragraph pairs, which are then presented to workers who write multi-hop questions over them [ce34e3bd9].

Each example carries supporting facts — a sentence-level supervision signal identifying the specific sentences that determine the answer, which the paper introduces as a distinguishing feature so systems can be trained and evaluated on explainability, not just the final answer [ce34e3bd9]. HotpotQA defines two evaluation settings: a distractor setting, where the 2 gold paragraphs are mixed with 8 retrieved distractor paragraphs and shuffled before being fed to the model, and a fullwiki setting, where the model must answer given the first paragraphs of all Wikipedia articles with no gold paragraphs specified — testing retrieval plus reasoning "in the wild" [ce34e3bd9].

Evaluation uses exact match (EM) and F1 as the base metrics, applied first to the answer span [ce34e3bd9]. A second set of EM/F1 metrics scores the predicted supporting-fact sentences against the gold set [ce34e3bd9]. A third, joint set combines answer and supporting-fact evaluation: joint precision and recall are the products of the answer and supporting-fact precision/recall (P(joint) = P(ans)·P(sup), R(joint) = R(ans)·R(sup)), and joint F1 is computed from those; joint EM is 1 only if both the answer and the supporting facts achieve an exact match, otherwise 0 [ce34e3bd9]. The paper explicitly encourages researchers to report all of these metrics when evaluating methods [ce34e3bd9].

## 2WikiMultiHopQA

2WikiMultiHopQA is a large multi-hop QA dataset with 192,606 total examples, built by combining structured Wikidata and unstructured Wikipedia text [cfbdd7c46]. Construction uses a pipeline of predefined templates over the intersection of each Wikipedia article's summary paragraph and the corresponding Wikidata statements: the process creates a set of templates, generates data, and post-processes it [cfbdd7c46]. Comparison-question templates were seeded by running NER over the comparison questions in HotpotQA's training data (17,456 questions) to abstract them into reusable templates, and templates that would yield single-hop or context-dependent questions were removed to ensure the questions remain genuinely multi-hop [cfbdd7c46].

The distinguishing supervision in 2WikiMultiHopQA is evidence information: a set of structured triples (subject entity, property, object entity) drawn from Wikidata that spell out the reasoning path from question to answer, which the paper frames as the key difference from HotpotQA — evidence that explains the reasoning path rather than only marking supporting sentences [cfbdd7c46]. The dataset has four question types — comparison (57,989 examples), inference (7,478), compositional (86,979), and bridge-comparison (40,160) [cfbdd7c46].

Evaluation mirrors HotpotQA's answer and sentence-level supporting-fact EM/F1, and adds an evidence-generation task that scores the predicted set of reasoning-path triples; the paper reports that the evidence-generation scores are very low (single-digit EM, low-teens F1 in the baseline), indicating that accurately recovering the full triple set is much harder than the binary sentence-level supporting-fact classification [cfbdd7c46].

## MuSiQue

MuSiQue is a multi-hop QA dataset of roughly 25K questions spanning 2-to-4 hops (24,814 total in the answerable variant, MuSiQue-Ans, split into train/dev/test and partitioned across 2-hop, 3-hop, and 4-hop questions) [cf13bf0c4]. (The exact per-split counts were not legible in this source's PDF→markdown table conversion, so only the verified 24,814 total is stated.) Its defining construction method is single-hop question composition: multi-hop questions are built bottom-up by selecting and composing single-hop questions drawn from existing datasets, assembling them into reasoning DAGs where the answer to one single-hop question feeds the next [cf13bf0c4].

The explicit design goal is to defeat disconnected-reasoning shortcuts. The paper's premise is that existing multi-hop benchmarks "are known to be largely solvable via shortcuts," and that a single multi-hop question alone is insufficient to force connected reasoning — a model can often find the correct answer from the context without actually resolving the intermediate sub-answer [cf13bf0c4]. To counter this, the pipeline minimizes overlap between train and dev/test (treating two multi-hop questions as overlapping if they share a single-hop question, a single-hop answer, or an associated paragraph) and composes questions so that arriving at the answer requires connected reasoning [cf13bf0c4]. The paper reports that the resulting answerable dataset has roughly 3x the human–machine gap and a substantially lower Disconnected Reasoning (DiRe) score — the metric quantifying how cheatable a dataset is — than HotpotQA and 2WikiMultiHopQA [cf13bf0c4].

MuSiQue is released in two variants: MuSiQue-Ans (the answerable set, with gold answers and supporting paragraphs from the construction pipeline) and MuSiQue-Full, which adds a paired unanswerable question for each answerable one — yielding ~50K questions of contrasting answerable/unanswerable pairs and an additional answerability-determination task [cf13bf0c4]. Evaluation uses Answer F1 and Support F1 (over the supporting paragraphs); MuSiQue-Full additionally requires the model to judge whether the context is sufficient to answer [cf13bf0c4].

## Synthesis

The three benchmarks form an escalating multi-hop-QA evaluation family along the axis of how strictly they force — and supervise — chained reasoning. HotpotQA establishes the template: crowdsourced multi-hop questions over Wikipedia paragraph pairs, with sentence-level supporting facts and a joint answer-plus-evidence metric [ce34e3bd9]. 2WikiMultiHopQA tightens the evidence signal from supporting sentences to structured Wikidata reasoning-path triples, making the reasoning chain itself an explicit, separately scored output [cfbdd7c46]. MuSiQue then attacks the failure mode the first two left open — that multi-hop questions are often solvable by disconnected shortcuts — by composing questions bottom-up from single-hop pieces, controlling train/test overlap, and adding unanswerable contrast pairs, and it reports a lower cheatability (DiRe) score than both predecessors as evidence this worked [cf13bf0c4].

This matters directly for a citation-gated research engine. Multi-hop is the structural shape of real research: the answer must be chained across multiple sources rather than read off one passage [ce34e3bd9][cf13bf0c4]. The supporting-fact and evidence-path supervision these datasets carry — sentence-level facts in HotpotQA, triple reasoning paths in 2WikiMultiHopQA, supporting paragraphs in MuSiQue — is exactly the per-claim grounding signal a citation-gated engine needs to evaluate, since each synthesized claim must point at the specific evidence that supports it, not just produce a plausible final answer [ce34e3bd9][cfbdd7c46][cf13bf0c4]. MuSiQue's disconnected-reasoning critique is the cautionary note: a system can appear to answer correctly while skipping the evidence chain, so grounding must be checked at the evidence level, not inferred from answer correctness [cf13bf0c4].
